What's happening

Floodwaters have swept through hillside communities

Days of relentless rain have flooded villages across Chittagong and Bandarban, submerging homes and cutting off entire hillside communities. Families are stranded without clean water, food, or dry shelter.

Amplitude volunteers are on the ground coordinating with local networks to move emergency supplies to the worst-hit areas — but the need is growing faster than we can meet it alone.

Where your donation goes

Every taka goes straight to the ground

  • Emergency food & clean water
    Dry rations and safe drinking water for stranded families
  • Shelter support
    Tarpaulins, dry clothing and bedding to keep families safe
  • Medical aid & first-aid kits
    Basic medicine and first-aid for injury and waterborne illness
  • On-ground coordination
    Working hand-in-hand with local volunteers to reach the worst-hit
